Open Dental → GoHighLevel Sync

Copies patients into GHL contacts and appointments into a GHL calendar. Back to data viewer

1. Patients → Contacts

Creates or updates a GHL contact for each patient: name, email, phone, birthday and address. Every contact gets the tag opendental and Contact Source opendental.

2. Appointments → Calendar

Creates or updates GHL appointments. Scheduled → confirmed, Complete → showed, Broken/Unscheduled → cancelled (and the contact gets the tag od-cancelled). Patients are added as contacts automatically. GHL notifications are turned off.

3. Automatic sync

Open Dental checks for changes every few seconds and sends them here straight away. New, moved, completed, broken or deleted appointments and new or edited patients go into GHL with no button to press. Workstation is the name of a computer at the practice that is always on and runs Open Dental (or the Open Dental API Service). Ask the practice for it: on that PC, Windows Settings → System → About → Device name.
